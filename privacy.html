GG Moments Clip Engine: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

GG Moments Clip Engine ("the app") is a private desktop application run by GG Moments on its owner's own computer. It is used only to manage the GG Moments channels and accounts.

What the app accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services. When the account owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the GG Moments YouTube channel,
  • read basic channel information (channel name and ID) and public YouTube data such as popular gaming videos, and
  • read YouTube Analytics for the channel's own videos (views, watch time, subscribers and, when available, estimated revenue).

How the data is used

This information is used only to upload GG Moments clips at scheduled times, to show which games are trending, and to show the channel's own performance inside the app.

How the data is stored

Sign-in tokens and channel data are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube. The app does not use cookies or tracking, and has no advertising.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties.

Third-party terms

By using YouTube features in this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also click "Disconnect" in the app, which deletes the stored sign-in from the computer. Stored data is deleted within 7 days of a disconnect or revoke, or immediately when the app is uninstalled.
